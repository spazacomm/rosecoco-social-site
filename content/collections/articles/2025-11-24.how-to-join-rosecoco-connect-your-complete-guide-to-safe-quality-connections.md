**Join Rosecoco Connect: Safe & Authentic Companionship**

At Rosecoco Connect, we bring together genuine people seeking meaningful connections in a safe, verified community. Every member goes through a verification process to ensure authenticity and quality interactions.

> Why Verification Matters
 Verification keeps the platform free from fake profiles and bots, giving you confidence that every interaction is with a real person. This creates a trustworthy environment for genuine connections.

How to Join
**Step 1: Sign Up**

Provide your:

- Email (for verification)
- Password
- Name
- Date of birth (18+)
- Location

Once submitted, verify your email to activate your account and access your dashboard.

**Step 2: Complete Your Profile**

A complete profile helps you stand out. Include:

- Photos: Clear headshot, lifestyle photos, full-body photo
- Bio: Short description of yourself and your interests
- Location & Lifestyle: Occupation, hobbies, values, companionship preferences

> Tip: Be authentic, specific, and show your personality. High-quality profiles get noticed faster.

**Step 3: Verify Your Identity**

_Selfie Verification (Required) _
Take a real-time selfie through our app to get the Verified Badge.

_ID Verification (Optional) _
Upload a government-issued ID along with a selfie holding it to earn the ID Verified Badge.

> Verified profiles receive higher visibility and increased engagement.

**Step 4: Profile Review**

Before going live, our team reviews your profile to ensure:

- Photos are authentic
- Profile is complete
- Community guidelines are met

> Approval usually takes 48–72 hours, with guidance provided if updates are needed.

**Step 5: Start Connecting**

Once approved:

- Your verified badge appears
- Enhanced search visibility
- Browse profiles, send connection requests, and chat securely
- Access safe tools to plan meetings
