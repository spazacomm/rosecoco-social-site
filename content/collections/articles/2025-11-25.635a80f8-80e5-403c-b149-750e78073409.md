Let me be straight with you – if you're still using directory sites in Kenya, you're playing with fire. I know they're convenient, and yeah, they've been the go-to for years. But after hearing enough horror stories from colleagues in Nairobi, Mombasa, and across the country, I had to make a change. Here's why directory sites are becoming a liability you can't afford.

**The Privacy Nightmare That's Getting Worse**
Directory sites are basically public databases of personal information, and that's terrifying. Your photos, phone number, rates, and personal details are all sitting there for anyone to grab, scrape, or screenshot. I've heard from too many people who've had their information leaked, shared without permission, or used by creeps for harassment.

Here's what happens: someone takes your photos from a directory site, posts them on social media as their own, and suddenly you're getting flooded with messages from people who think you're something else entirely. Your personal safety becomes compromised, and good luck trying to get that information removed.

**The Verification Problem (Or Complete Lack Thereof)**
Directory sites don't verify anyone. Period. That means anyone can list themselves, claim to be working in Karen while actually living in Kasarani, or pretend to have professional photos when they're using stolen images. For legitimate service providers, this creates a nightmare scenario where quality and safety standards are completely ignored.

When potential clients scroll through directory listings, they have no way to know if the person they're contacting is actually who they claim to be. This leads to countless time-wasters, fake inquiries, and dangerous situations that could have been prevented with proper verification.

**The Scam Epidemic**
The scam problems on directory sites are out of control. Clients can easily fake profiles, steal photos, and create multiple listings to waste your time or set up dangerous situations. There are whole networks of people who treat directory sites like hunting grounds for free services, false bookings, and outright fraud.

I've personally dealt with clients who found my directory listing, arranged meetings, then tried to negotiate rates down by 70% or insisted on "trying before paying." When you work through unverified directory platforms, you have zero protection against these situations.

**Business Reputation Damage**
One bad experience can destroy your reputation when you're listed on directory sites. Since there's no professional verification or quality control, one person's negative experience can spread quickly across online forums and social media groups. Without proper verification systems, your business reputation becomes vulnerable to fake reviews, malicious posts, and unfounded allegations.

Directory sites also make it impossible to build a professional brand. When you're lumped together with everyone else in a generic listing, potential clients have no way to distinguish legitimate professionals from time-wasters and low-quality operators.
